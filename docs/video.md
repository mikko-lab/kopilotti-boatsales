# BoatSales introduction video

The approved product film is available on the [BoatSales homepage](https://boats.kopilotti.online/#video) and as an [MP4 download](../site/media/boatsales-introduction-2026-09.mp4).

- Runtime: approximately 1 minute 13 seconds.
- English synthetic narration and English subtitles included in the image.
- The homepage includes the English transcript. An additional caption overlay is not enabled, to avoid duplicate subtitles.
- The film illustrates the planned customer journey. BoatSales is in pilot preparation; this is not a recording of an operational sales service.
- The pictured vessels are illustrative, not BoatSales listings or customer references.
