Who is BoatSales for?

The first pilot is intended for professional boat dealers and yacht brokers. It is a dedicated marine application within the Kopilotti Sales family.

Does every boat need an inspection report?

Yes. A completed condition inspection and an existing report are required before a vessel can be offered through BoatSales. The buyer must be able to review the report.

Who controls the price and the sale?

The seller defines the commercial terms and remains responsible for the sale. Questions or conditions that need personal attention are handled by the seller’s team.

Who handles payment and financing?

The buyer pays the seller directly through the arrangements agreed with the seller. Financing is arranged with the seller and their chosen finance provider. BoatSales does not receive or hold the purchase funds or grant financing.

When is the success fee charged?

The proposed fee is based on the final sale price and is triggered by the seller’s confirmation that the full purchase payment has been received. The exact rate, taxes and commercial terms are agreed before the pilot.

Can a buyer offer a boat in part exchange?

Trade-ins are outside the initial BoatSales release. Any such request needs a separate conversation with the selling business.

Is BoatSales available internationally?

BoatSales is being developed for international boat sales. Pilot availability, supported markets and local sales requirements are agreed individually before use. This website introduces the service; it does not accept purchase offers or payments.

How do we get started?

Contact us with your business, your market and the types of vessels you sell. Together we can select a small starting inventory, agree responsibilities and define a suitable pilot.
